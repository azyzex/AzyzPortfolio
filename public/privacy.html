Privacy Notice

How this site handles your data

Last updated: June 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Mohamed Aziz Guenni. It is a simple showcase site. The only place it collects personal information is the contact form — and only if you choose to use it. This page explains what happens to that information.

What I collect

When you send a message through the contact form, I receive the details you type in:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The contents of your message

I don't ask for anything else, and I don't collect data in the background: this site uses no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies.

How your message reaches me

Your message is delivered to me by email — either through a form-handling service (Formspree, which forwards submissions to my inbox) or, as a fallback, by opening a pre-filled draft in your own email app. In both cases the message ends up in my email account (Google Gmail). These providers process the data on my behalf according to their own privacy policies.

Why I use it

Only to read and reply to what you sent me. I don't use your details for marketing, and I don't sell or rent them to anyone.

How long I keep it

I keep your message for as long as it's useful for our conversation and any follow-up. You can ask me to delete it at any time (see below).

Your choices and rights

You can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under the GDPR, or Tunisia under Law No. 2004-63), you may have additional rights. To make any such request, just email me.
